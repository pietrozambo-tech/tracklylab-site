Track Apple Pay payments automatically on iPhone (Shortcuts)

Every time you pay with Apple Pay in a shop, your iPhone already knows the amount and the shop. One Shortcuts automation hands them to your expense tracker, so the expense is there before you have put your phone away - with no bank login and nothing sent to anyone.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later - the Wallet automation in Shortcuts arrived with iOS 17.
  • A payment card in Wallet that you use with Apple Pay.
  • TracklyLab installed: it adds the Record payment action to Shortcuts. It is free, with no ads.

Watch it done

Recorded on an iPhone 16 Pro with iOS 27.2, in English. On other versions some names or places may differ a little - the steps below say what to look for.

Set it up, step by step

About two minutes, once. You can also follow the same steps inside the app: Settings › Apple Pay automation.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and find Automation: depending on your iOS version it is a tab at the bottom, an item in the Library, or the Automation filter above the actions when you tap +.
  2. Create a new automation (+ or New Automation) and choose Wallet (“When I tap any of … Wallet passes or payment cards”). On some versions it is called Transaction.
  3. In the trigger, under cards, pick only your payment cards: not transit, tickets or loyalty cards, so it does not run when you tap a pass. Any merchant is fine.
  4. Choose Run Immediately (not “Run After Confirmation”) and turn Notify When Run off. If you do not see them, they are under the arrow next to the trigger.
  5. If asked, choose New Blank Automation. Then search for TracklyLab and add Record payment under the trigger.
  6. Tap Amount, then Select Variable above the keyboard. A blue variable appears under the trigger: tap it and it goes into the field. It reads Transaction or, on some iOS versions, Shortcut Input. Then tap the variable now in the field and choose Amount.
  7. Do the same for Merchant (choose Merchant) and, under the arrow on the action, for Card (choose Card or Pass). Go back: it is saved.

What happens when you pay

  • You pay with Apple Pay in a shop, as always.
  • A notification arrives with the expense ready: €12.40 · Café Central - Tap to track it ⚡️. Tap it, pick a category, save.
  • The second time you pay at the same place, the category and account are already chosen - and if you like, the app saves it by itself, with Undo right in the notification.
  • Busy? The payments wait in the app until you look at them.

What it does not catch

  • Online and in-app Apple Pay does not trigger the automation - only payments in a shop do. Those you add by hand, in two taps.
  • A declined payment you retry within a minute is counted once; one you do not retry, you discard from the list or with Undo.
  • With no signal the payment usually arrives when you are back online, with your bank’s own notification. When Wallet gets it very late (poor signal, some merchants) and shows it with the earlier time, iOS sometimes does not run the automation: add that one by hand.

Questions

Does it connect to my bank?

No. The amount, the shop and the card name go from Wallet to the app on your iPhone, through Shortcuts. There is no bank login and no third party in between.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a card set up for Apple Pay. The Wallet automation is part of Shortcuts, which comes with iOS.

Does it work for online payments?

No - iOS runs the automation only when you pay in a shop. Online and in-app payments you add by hand.

Will it ask me every time?

Not if you choose Run Immediately and turn Notify When Run off (step 4). Then the only thing you see is TracklyLab’s notification with the expense ready.

Is TracklyLab free?

Yes, with no ads. It also works in the browser, with no account, on any phone or computer - the Apple Pay automation needs the iPhone app.
