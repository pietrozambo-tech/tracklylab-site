And there's more

Everything a month needs.Nothing it doesn't.

A year of statements, in a minute

A bank statement, a spreadsheet, a PDF or a screenshot: it is read for you, matched to your categories, and nothing comes in until you've checked it. Splitwise and Tricount exports too.

Shared, with your partner

Mark an expense as shared and it shows on both phones, split your way. The balance keeps itself - and says whose turn it is to get dinner.

A budget that knows you

A budget bar that compares you with your own usual month, not with an arbitrary target - and tells you, mid-month, whether you're on track.

Accounts and balances

Cash, cards and bank accounts: what you have right now, on which account, and how it changes over time - with transfers between them and balances to check against the bank.

Find anything

Every transaction, searchable and filterable by date, category and account. Export it all to CSV, any time.

Made with care

In English and Italian, on any iPhone - the smallest included. And in any browser, on any phone or computer.

Savings by group

Put a theme's income and spending together - the rent you collect, what the home costs - and see what it really nets, month by month.

The month, summed up

When a month ends, its summary is waiting: against your usual, what weighed most, and how it would have gone without the biggest expense.

Recurring, on their own

Rent, subscriptions, salary: set up once what repeats, and it is added every week, month or year - and you see what is coming up.

Paid for everyone?

Log just your share, or the total and then the refunds as they come in: who owes you what, with no maths by hand.

Light by day, dark at night

Light, dark, or following your iPhone - every screen is made for both.
