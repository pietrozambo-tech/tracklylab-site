See how, in one minute.

The automation lives in Apple's Shortcuts app. Here it is done on a real iPhone, step by step - follow along, then pay with Apple Pay and the expense is already in TracklyLab.

The video is on its wayMeanwhile, the written steps are in the app: Settings › Apple Pay automation.
  1. 1Shortcuts → Automation → New automation → Wallet.
  2. 2Pick only your payment cards, any merchant.
  3. 3Run Immediately, and Notify When Run off.
  4. 4Add TracklyLab's Record payment: Amount, Merchant and Card from the Transaction.
