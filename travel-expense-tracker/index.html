Track a trip’s expenses in any currency - and see what it really cost

A trip is spread over months - the flights booked in spring, the hotel, the dinners abroad - and often over more than one currency. In TracklyLab a trip is a name on its expenses: it adds them up on its own, and each one is converted at the rate of the day you paid.

A trip, in one place

  • Give it a name and, if you like, its dates. Then pick the expenses it already has - the flights booked months earlier included - or add them as you go.
  • Its total splits by what the money went on: flights, hotel, food, activities.
  • Every trip side by side, year by year: what this one cost, against the last.
  • Travelled with friends who kept a Splitwise or Tricount? Import the export and only your share comes in.

In any currency

  • Abroad, the app offers the local currency first when you add an expense: in New York, the dollar.
  • Spend in any of 155 currencies. Each expense keeps its own and is converted at that day’s rate, then fixed there: the trip stays what it really cost you, whatever the rate does afterwards.
  • Your totals, budget and charts stay in your own currency.

Less typing on the road

  • Apple Pay in shops logs itself: pay with your iPhone and the expense arrives ready, amount and place included.
  • Everything else in two taps: amount, category, done.
  • Back home with a pile of receipts? Import the bank statement and check each row before it comes in.

Your month, still honest

A trip’s expenses count in the month you paid them, so a holiday shows up where it really hit. Paid a big one in one go? Spread it over 3, 6 or 12 months and each month carries its part.

Questions

Does it convert the currencies for me?

Yes. Pick the currency when you add the expense - abroad, the local one is offered first - and it is converted into yours at that day’s rate.

What if the rate moves after the trip?

Nothing changes: each expense keeps the rate of the day you paid, so the trip’s total stays what it really cost.

Can I add a trip I have already taken?

Yes. Make the trip and pick the expenses it already has, or import a bank statement, a Splitwise or a Tricount export.

Can I make it before I leave?

Yes: give it a name and its dates now, and add the expenses as you book and pay.
