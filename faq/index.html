Apple Pay

Does it log online Apple Pay payments too?

Not by itself: iOS runs the automation only when you pay in a shop. Online and in-app payments you add in two taps.

What do I need for the Apple Pay automation?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a card in Wallet. You set up one automation in Shortcuts, once - the app walks you through it.

Privacy and your account

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, never. Expenses come in from Apple Pay, in two taps, or from a statement you choose to import. TracklyLab never asks for a bank login.

Why sign in?

Sign in with Apple, Google or your email and your expenses are backed up and in sync on your iPhone, your computer and any browser - and they come back with you on a new phone.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. Your expenses are yours: no ads, no data sold. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

Every day

Can I use it without an iPhone?

Yes: TracklyLab works in any browser, on Android phones and on computers. An Android app is planned for 2027. The widgets and the Apple Pay automation are iPhone only.

Can I bring in what I tracked before?

Yes, with an account: a bank statement, a spreadsheet, a PDF or a screenshot is read automatically and matched to your categories, and nothing is added until you’ve checked it. Splitwise and Tricount exports too.

Can I share expenses with my partner or a flatmate?

Yes. Connect with a code: shared expenses show on both phones, split your way, only your share counts in your budget, and the balance updates by itself.

Does it work for trips abroad?

Yes. Spend in over 150 currencies: each expense is converted at the day’s rate and fixed there, so a trip stays what it really cost you.

I paid dinner for everyone: how do I log it?

Just your share, or the total and then the refunds as they come in - you choose once, in Profile › Paid for others.

What about a big yearly payment?

Spread it over 3, 6 or 12 months and each month’s budget counts its part - the insurance, a new laptop, the car tax.

Can I keep receipts, say for my tax return?

Yes, with an account: snap the receipt or attach the invoice when you log the expense, and it’s there when you need it. At year end, export the year’s receipts - all of them, or only some categories such as Health - as one PDF with the total, or as a ZIP with a CSV list.

Can I set a budget for just one category?

Yes: in Settings › Budget, give a monthly budget to the categories you choose. The Dashboard shows the ones close to their limit, and tells you once when one is reached.
