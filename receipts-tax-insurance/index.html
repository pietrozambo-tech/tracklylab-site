Receipts

Receipts kept with your expenses, ready for your tax return and your insurer

Medicines at the pharmacy, the dentist, new glasses, the laptop still under warranty: over a year the receipts end up in a drawer, and the drawer at the bottom of a bag - just when the tax return, the insurer or the office asks for them. In TracklyLab the receipt’s photo stays with the expense it belongs to, and at the end of the year the ones you need come out together in one file.

Snap it when you log it

  1. In Add, tap the receipt button beside the date and take the photo - or pick one from your library.
  2. The receipt shows under the date. Tap it to see it full screen, replace it, share it or remove it.
  3. Save. The photo is made smaller on your phone first - about 200 KB, still sharp enough to read every line - and kept in your account.

Find them in a second

Every expense with a receipt carries a small mark in Activity. Filters › Only with a receipt lists just those - for a month, a year or everything.

At the end of the year, one file

Settings › Data › Export receipts. Pick the year and the categories you need - Health for your tax return, say, or only Pharmacy and Doctors. The sheet tells you how many receipts and how much before you export.

  • PDF: a first page with the list, dates, amounts and the total, then one receipt per page, centred at the size of a real receipt. Ready to send, print or attach.
  • ZIP: a folder with every photo, named by date, place and amount (“2026-11-12 Riverside Pharmacy 18.40€.jpg”), and the list as a CSV that opens in any spreadsheet.

When it helps

  • Your tax return: medicines, specialist visits, the dentist, glasses and lenses, physiotherapy - every medical receipt of the year in one place when it is time to claim them.
  • Health insurance or a health fund: send the receipts for a reimbursement without hunting for them.
  • Work expenses you pay first and claim back with an expense report.
  • Warranties and returns: the receipt of the new laptop or the washing machine is where the expense is, years later.

Where the photos live

  • Receipts need an account: the photo is kept in private storage that only your account can open, and follows you to every device you sign in on.
  • The expense carries only a reference to it, so syncing stays as light as before.
  • Removing the receipt, deleting the expense, erasing your data or deleting your account deletes the photo too. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Receipts are kept in your account, so they need one - Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code. Without an account, TracklyLab keeps everything on your phone and offers no receipts.

Can I add the receipt to an expense I already logged?

Yes: open the expense, tap the receipt button beside the date, take or pick the photo, and save.

How much space does a receipt take?

About 200 KB: the photo is made smaller on your phone before it leaves, sharp enough to read every line. A photo straight from the camera is often 5 to 15 MB.

Can the AI import turn a receipt photo into an expense?

Yes. Import the photo in Settings › Import data: it becomes the expense, and you can keep the photo with it as its receipt, with one switch.

Can I export only the medical receipts?

Yes. Pick your Health category - or only some subcategories, such as Pharmacy - when you export.
