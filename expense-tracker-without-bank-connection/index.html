An expense tracker that doesn’t connect to your bank

Many expense trackers start by asking for your bank login, so a third party can read your accounts. TracklyLab never asks for it. The expenses still get in - most of them on their own - and you decide what leaves your phone.

Why skip the bank connection

  • No bank credentials, and no access to your accounts, handed to an app or to the aggregator behind it.
  • Nothing to reconnect when a bank changes its login or the link expires.
  • Only what you choose to record is there - not every movement of every account.

How the expenses get in, then

  • Apple Pay, on its own: one Shortcuts automation on the iPhone, and every payment in a shop arrives with amount and place ready; at places it knows, it saves itself.
  • Everything else in two taps: amount, category, done. The app remembers your choices and offers them next time.
  • Recurring payments - rent, subscriptions, salary - add themselves on the right day.
  • A statement when you want one: import a bank statement, a spreadsheet, a PDF or a screenshot, and check every row before anything is added. Splitwise and Tricount exports too.

Where your data lives

  • Without an account - in the iPhone app or in the browser - your data stays on your device.
  • With an account (Sign in with Apple, Google or an email code) it is backed up and synced across your devices, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.
  • Importing a file needs an account: the file is read by an AI model (Anthropic’s Claude) to file each row, and it is not kept. The app asks your permission first.
  • Export a full backup or a CSV whenever you like. No ads, and no data sold to anyone.

The trade-off, honestly

Without a bank connection, payments that are not Apple Pay in a shop - a physical card, an online purchase, a transfer - do not arrive by themselves. You add them in two taps, or bring in a statement now and then and the app files the rows for you.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. TracklyLab works without one, in the iPhone app and in the browser, and your data stays on your device. An account adds backup, sync across devices and file import.

Can I still import my bank statement?

Yes, as a file you download from your bank - a statement, an export, a spreadsheet, a PDF or a screenshot. You check every row before anything is added. This needs an account.

Is it on Android?

The web app works in any browser, on Android too, with no install. An Android app is planned for 2027.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads.

Try it without signing up

Open it in your browser, or install the iPhone app. No bank login, no account needed.
