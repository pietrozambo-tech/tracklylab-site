Questions

Good to know,before you start.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, never. Expenses come in from Apple Pay, in two taps, or from a statement you choose to import. TracklyLab never asks for a bank login.

What do I need for the Apple Pay automation?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a card in Wallet. You set up one automation in Shortcuts, once - the app walks you through it.

Can I use it without an iPhone?

Yes: TracklyLab works in any browser, on Android phones and on computers. An Android app is coming in 2027. The widgets and the Apple Pay automation are iPhone only.

Can I share expenses with my partner or a flatmate?

Yes. Connect with a code: shared expenses show on both phones, split your way, only your half counts in your budget, and the balance keeps itself.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. Your expenses are yours: no ads, nothing sold. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.
