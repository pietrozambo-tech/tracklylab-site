Shared expenses

Shared expenses with your partner or flatmate, without a spreadsheet

Rent, the weekly shop, the bills. In TracklyLab each of you enters your own expenses on your own phone; the ones marked shared reach the other one too, only your half counts in your budget, and the balance between you keeps itself.

How it works

  • You pay for the groceries, 84€: you enter 84€ and mark it shared. 42€ lands in your budget, and the balance says the other person owes you 42€.
  • They pay the electricity: it appears on your phone too, and the balance moves the other way.
  • Settle up when you like - one tap, the balance returns to zero, and your spending, income and budget don’t move.
  • You each see only the expenses marked shared, never the other’s private ones.

Getting connected

  • With a code: one of you creates it and sends it, the other types it in. Both need an account, so the shared expenses can reach both phones.
  • On your own, for now: if the other person does not use the app, track the split and the balance on your phone alone, and connect whenever they join.

Make it automatic

  • Categories that split on their own: choose Housing or Groceries once, and every expense in them is shared without a tap.
  • Apple Pay: pay the shop with your iPhone and the expense arrives ready - shared, if its category is.
  • Recurring payments - the rent on the 1st - add themselves on the right day.

Your budget stays yours

  • Your budget, trends and monthly summary count your share - what you actually spent - not the household’s.
  • When you want the whole picture, the shared page shows the full household amounts.
  • Paid a dinner for a group, once? Log just your share, or the total and then the refunds as they come.

Good to know

Shared expenses are for two people: you and a partner or a flatmate, split in half. For a group trip with several friends, import the Splitwise or Tricount export and only your share comes in.

Questions

Does the other person need the app?

No. You can track the split and the balance on your phone alone. If they install it too, you connect with a code and the shared expenses reach both phones.

Do we see each other’s expenses?

Only the ones marked shared. Everything else stays private to each of you.

Does it work with Apple Pay?

Yes. With the Apple Pay automation, a payment in a shop arrives ready to save, and it is shared if its category splits on its own.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads.
